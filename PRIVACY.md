# Privacy Policy — Stay on Monitor

Stay on Monitor does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- The only thing saved is your setting (on/off and the chosen monitor), stored locally in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`.
- The extension makes no network requests.
- `system.display` permission is used only to know where your monitors are, so windows can be moved back to the chosen one.
- Donation links in the popup open external sites (Ko-fi, PayPal, GitHub, Pix donation page) only when you click them.

Contact: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
